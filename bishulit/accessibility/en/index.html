Accessibility Statement: Bishulit

Effective: 21 November 2026 · Developer: Limony Labs, an independent developer based in Israel · Contact: limonylabs@gmail.com · Address: https://limonylabs.github.io/bishulit/accessibility

We want Bishulit to be comfortable for everyone who cooks. This statement describes what was actually tested in version 0.1.0, what has not been tested yet, and how to reach us. It is factual and is not a statement of conformance with a standard.

What was tested

Tests were run on an Android emulator as part of the app's quality checks:

  • Hebrew and right-to-left: every screen is in Hebrew and right-to-left, including cooking mode. The app interface is in Hebrew only.
  • Text size: the app follows the system font size up to 200%. The library, recipe, week, shopping and editor screens were tested at that size.
  • Dark theme: light and dark themes are available.
  • Touch targets: every tested button or tappable item is at least 48dp.
  • Screen reader labels: every tested tappable item has a label. Editor fields are announced as "ingredient N" and "step N" (in Hebrew), and drag handles as "drag to reorder".
  • Cooking mode: one step at a time, in large text, with the screen kept on.
  • Keyboard and arrows: every action can be reached with the arrow keys.

Not tested yet, or known to be partial

  • Full TalkBack pass:. Labels were checked with an automated tool, but no full screen-reader walkthrough was done.
  • Colour contrast:. The main design colours were checked separately and meet at least 4.5:1, but not every screen was measured.
  • TAB key navigation: partial. In some library states TAB does not reach the bottom navigation bar; the arrow keys do.
  • Conformance with the Israeli standard IS 5568 has not been assessed.

Accessibility contact

Having trouble? Write to limonylabs@gmail.com and describe the screen, what you tried to do and your device's accessibility settings. We will try to reply and fix it in the next version.
