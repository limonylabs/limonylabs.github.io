Privacy Policy: Bishulit

Effective: 21 November 2026 · App version: 0.1.0 and later
Developer: Limony Labs, an independent developer based in Israel · Contact: limonylabs@gmail.com · Policy address: https://limonylabs.github.io/bishulit/privacy

In short

Bishulit is a recipe book that works on your device. There is no sign-up and no account, and we have no server. The app sends us no information. The only information that reaches us is what you choose to send us by email.

What is stored, and where

  • Your recipes, collections, week plan, shopping list and settings are stored only in the app's storage on your device.
  • Recipe images load from the site you saved the recipe from. So they show quickly and offline, a copy is kept in the app's cache on your device. The copy is not sent anywhere, and it is deleted when you clear the app's cache in Android settings or uninstall the app.

When the app uses the internet

Only in two cases:

  1. When you import a recipe from a link you chose, the app downloads that page from the site.
  2. When a recipe image is shown, it loads from the original site.

In both cases the request goes directly from your device to that site, like normal browsing, not through us. The request identifies itself as Bishulit (in the User-Agent), and the site sees an ordinary request (for example your IP address and device type). Its use of that information is governed by its own privacy policy. We never receive the request or its content.

"Open site" and "Share" take you to the browser or app you choose. Sharing sends only the recipe name and link. What happens there is governed by that browser's or app's policy.

What the app does not have

  • No account, sign-up or login.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics, tracking, server crash reporting or third-party libraries of that kind.
  • No permissions for contacts, location, camera, microphone or storage. The only permission is internet access, for imports and images. A backup file is opened only when you pick it.
  • No in-app purchases.

Contacting us by email

If you write to us at limonylabs@gmail.com, we receive your email address, the content of your message and any attachments. We use them only to reply to you. Contacting us is optional, and the app works without it. The mailbox is hosted on Google's Gmail. Your messages and details are deleted once no longer needed to handle your request, and within 12 months at most. You can ask to see, correct or delete it at the same address.

Data security

  • Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot normally access. Its protection also depends on your device's screen lock.
  • A backup file you export is not encrypted. Keep it somewhere safe and share it only with people you trust.

Backup

  • Android Auto Backup: if it is on for your device, Android keeps a copy of the recipe database in your Google backup account, and also moves it to a new device when you switch devices, according to your settings. The cache and images are not included. You control this in Android settings, and we never receive the copy.
  • Export backup: you can export your recipes to a file and choose where to save it or whom to share it with. The file contains your recipes and image addresses, not the images themselves. After export, the file is under your control.

Retention and deletion

We keep no information from the app. Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app, and the cache is cleared by Android or when you clear it.

  • "Delete all data" in Settings removes recipes, the week plan and the shopping list from the device (with a double confirmation).
  • Uninstalling the app removes all its data from the device, including the cache.
  • A copy in Android backup is managed in your Google account.

Your rights

Because we hold no information about you from the app, there is nothing for us to show you or delete. For emails you sent us, you can ask to see, correct or delete them at limonylabs@gmail.com.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not designed for or directed at children.
